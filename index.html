publications news CV

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (IPADS), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Haibo Chen and Prof. Mingkai Dong. I received my B.Eng. degree in Information Engineering from SJTU in 2025.

My research interests primarily focus on MLsys, AI Infrastructure, and Operating Systems. Currently, I am working on optimizing system mismatches in on-device LLM inference and agentic systems.

I am a core contributor to PowerServe, a high-performance LLM inference framework for mobile devices.
