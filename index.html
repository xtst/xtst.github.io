Research Focus

My research sits at the intersection of AI and systems. I primarily focus on two complementary directions:

01

Systems for AI workloads

Efficient infrastructure for AI workloads, from on-device inference with Sereno to large-scale LLM reinforcement learning with UniExact.

02

Systems where AI is the author

Correctness and long-term maintenance when AI writes system artifacts, from LLM-derived knowledge with LLMake to file-system code with FSCrash.

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (并行与分布式系统研究所, IPADS), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (上海交通大学, SJTU), advised by Haibo Chen (陈海波) and Mingkai Dong (董明凯). I received my B.Eng. degree in Information Engineering from SJTU in 2025.

Across both directions, I work at the intersection of ML systems and operating systems, with an emphasis on dependable, efficient, and deployable systems.
