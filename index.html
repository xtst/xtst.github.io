Current Research

I build infrastructure for AI agents and model training, with a current emphasis on efficient post-training systems for LLMs and multimodal LLMs.

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (IPADS), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Haibo Chen and Prof. Mingkai Dong. I received my B.Eng. degree in Information Engineering from SJTU in 2025.

Previously, I worked on systems for on-device LLM inference, including resource management and high-performance inference runtimes. My broader interests span ML systems and operating systems.
